How can we help?

search
How do I track my order? expand_more

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to our carrier's website. You can also view the status of your order directly in your Account Dashboard under the "Order History" section. Tracking updates usually begin appearing 24-48 hours after shipment.

What is your return policy? expand_more

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. If you aren't completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return items in their original, unwashed, and unworn condition with tags attached. Please note that certain items, such as final sale items and intimates, are not eligible for return.

Do you ship internationally? expand_more

Yes, we currently ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by location and will be calculated at checkout. Please be aware that duties and taxes may be applied by your local customs office upon arrival.

How can I change my shipping address? expand_more

If your order hasn't been processed yet, we may be able to update your shipping address. Please contact our support team immediately at support@minimalistshop.com with your order number. Once an order has been fulfilled, we are unable to redirect it.

What payments do you accept? expand_more

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), as well as PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We also offer buy-now-pay-later options through Klarna and Afterpay in select regions.

Still need help?

Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm EST to assist you with any questions.
